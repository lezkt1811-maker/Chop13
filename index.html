    How to Use

    1. Audio Source: The app starts with a built-in demo beat. Search for a song in Find & Import Songs and click Import, or upload your own MP3/WAV with the top-right button.

    2. Screwed Speed & Pitch: Drag the pitch/speed slider down between 0.75x and 0.85x for the slowed-down H-Town vibe.

    3. Bass Boost: Pump the sub frequencies around 80Hz with the bass slider.

    4. Chopped Stutter Pad: Hold the chop pad during playback to repeat a short slice at the playhead.

    5. Vinyl Stop: Click the vinyl tape stop button for a turntable power-off effect.

    Built with Tailwind CSS, Tone.js & Web Audio API. Runs entirely in your browser.
